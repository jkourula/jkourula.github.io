Turn-Based Racing

Top-down racing where every move is a decision.

  • Each turn, the coloured fan in front of your car shows every move you can make.
  • Distance from the car is your speed: the near edge is full braking, the far edge is full throttle.
  • Width is how much you can steer. The faster you go, the narrower it gets.
  • Green moves keep grip. Yellow moves slide: you turn harder but might spin out. Red moves are even sharper and much more likely to spin. The further out you click, the higher the risk.
  • Grass slows you down. All cars move at the same time.
  • Hitting walls or other cars damages your car. A damaged car is slower and has less grip. At 100% damage the car is wrecked.
